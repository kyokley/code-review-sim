Average review lifecycle

Each image shows averages across trials, not an actual sampled run. Fractional revised-line averages are expected. Rows are mean review-ordinal blocks, not a fixed partition. Agile blocks have fresh random binomial sizes with nominal mean shown in the configuration. Fractional boundaries are averages; missing tail reviews contribute boundaries at the project end, zero lines, and zero effort. Vertical milestones show mean code progress; horizontal position is serial effort in time units. Both images use the same effort scale. Stage order: Writing → Initial review → Revision → Follow-up review.

Read this as a model, not a forecast. Results are serial total effort in normalized time units, not elapsed calendar time. Inputs are illustrative, not measured productivity data. Shared per-line revision outcomes pair the strategies. The model omits defect propagation, context-switch costs, and benefits of earlier feedback; it cannot establish a universal best workflow. The confidence interval is uncertainty in mean paired savings, not the range containing 95% of projects.
